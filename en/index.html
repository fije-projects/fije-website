Services

Where Fije can help

Every project starts from a different situation. These are the most common — if yours is different, tell us about it.

Assessment and technical review

Something isn't working, but it's not clear where to start

The org has become hard to change, processes have grown tangled, manual steps or technical debt remain.

How Fije helps

It reviews the system, architecture and processes, and compares the possible solutions by impact, time and cost.

A concrete assessment to decide on: what to change, what to leave, in what order and with what effort.

Salesforce development and architecture

You have a solution to build or an org to evolve

Customisations and integrations have piled up over time, and every change risks adding more.

How Fije helps

It designs and builds starting from the process to support: sales and service, automation, integrations, data, security and access.

A solution that meets today's need without making tomorrow's harder.

Technical guidance and delivery support

You need a senior technical reference

Decisions to make, vendor proposals to assess or a complex project to steer, without a dedicated senior person in-house.

How Fije helps

It translates business needs into technical choices, reviews architectures and estimates, and supports delivery.

Sounder decisions, and an independent point of reference throughout the project.

Beyond Salesforce

If the problem spans several systems, we look with you for the simplest solution, beyond Salesforce too: integrations, automation and custom tools.

Approach

We work it out together

No off-the-shelf solutions: every step is shared with you and your team, so the choices still make sense long after the project ends.

  1. 1

    Understand

    We start from your situation: together we pin down the problem, the goal and the constraints — before writing a line of code.

  2. 2

    Simplify

    We separate what's really needed from the complexity built up over time, and set the priorities together — no decisions handed down.

  3. 3

    Build

    We deliver a solution proportionate to the problem, with your team involved along the way — so whoever uses and maintains it knows how it works.

Behind Fije

A name that is a way of working

Fije is first of all a way of approaching problems together: finding the thread that runs through them and following it, with you, to a workable solution.

Today it is a deliberately lean operation: most of the experience and delivery comes from Klodjan Meta. The aim is for Fije to grow as an identity and a way of working, beyond a single person.
