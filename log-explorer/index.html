Free Chrome extension · Salesforce

Salesforce debug logs, without the trip through Setup.

Fije Log Explorer opens the latest logs of the org you're working on in their own tab. You can search inside them, read them with colour-coding, and turn logging on for a user in a couple of clicks.

What it does

From "something broke" to the line that explains it.

Find the right log

The extension opens on the org's 30 most recent logs and starts downloading their contents right away, so there's no empty form to fill in.

  • Filter by user and time window.
  • Full-text search inside the logs. Type a method, an exception or an Id and see the matching lines.
  • Auto-refresh while you re-run the transaction.
  • Exception count and a flag for truncated logs.

Read it without getting lost

Even multi-megabyte logs open instantly in a viewer built for reading them.

  • Colour-coding by category: errors, debug, DML / SOQL, callouts, flow, limits.
  • Indentation by call depth, with categories you can show or hide.
  • Recognised Salesforce Ids become clickable links.
  • Find in log, copy, download.

Switch logging on in two clicks

See every active trace flag in the org in one table, and enable or extend logging for a user by 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

  • Uses a dedicated debug level, or one that already exists in your org.
  • Disable it as soon as you're done.

Several orgs open? No problem

If you have a sandbox and production open in different tabs, you pick which org to work on. Enhanced Domains are handled automatically.

Privacy

Your logs stay between you and your org.

The extension talks only to the Salesforce org you're already signed in to, using the session you already have.

  • No backendThere's no Fije server: everything runs in your browser.
  • Nothing collectedNo sign-up, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Nothing storedLogs live in memory in the tab and are discarded when you close it.

Details and permissions in the privacy policy.

How to use it

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome.
  2. Open your Salesforce org in a tab and sign in as usual.
  3. Click the Fije Log Explorer icon. A new tab opens on the most recent logs.
