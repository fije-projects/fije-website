Fije Log Explorer for Salesforce

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

In short: this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or business data to third parties. There is no server operated by the author — it runs entirely in your browser.

Data handled

  • Salesforce session (sid): the extension reads the session cookie of the Salesforce org you already have open in a tab, and uses it as an Authorization: Bearer token to call the Tooling/REST API of that same org. The token is never written to disk nor sent anywhere other than your org's own Salesforce domains (*.salesforce.com, *.force.com).
  • Debug log content and user records fetched from the org live only in memory in the extension's tab and are discarded when the tab is closed or a new search is run. Nothing is persisted — chrome.storage is not used.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
